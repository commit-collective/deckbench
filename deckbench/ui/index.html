Decks on the network

Behaviour

Strict decksServe the players 1 to 4 that announced themselves and nobody else, as a CDJ-2000NXS2 or an XDJ-1000MK2 does.
Refuse the databaseTurn metadata requests down, as real decks do those of a device that is not a player.
Claim the number firstA deck that comes on does what a player does: three hellos and the three stages of claiming its number (3.6 s), and only then its keep-alives. Off, it is there at once.
ModelThe player the decks stand in for: its name, the length of its status packets and a byte of them.
Model nameWhat the decks announce themselves as (20 characters at most).

Connect to a bench

This page drives a deckbench that runs on your network, on the machine that is cabled to the players. Start it there with:

python -m deckbench --http-host 0.0.0.0 --allow-origin ORIGIN --token auto
